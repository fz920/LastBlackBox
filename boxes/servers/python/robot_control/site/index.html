How conversation works

Speak near the robot while holding the button, then release to send. Up to 15 seconds per recording or 500 characters per typed question. Keyboard: focus Hold to talk and hold Enter.

Typed questions do not record the microphone. End conversation stops the reply and clears its context. Leaving the page cancels the turn. Remembers up to six questions; clears after two idle minutes.

Requires internet and paid API usage. Audio and images stay in memory on the Pi, not in recording files. This mode does not control movement. Preview and offline descriptions stay local.

Search limits and privacy

Your typed clue or released recording, search criteria, recent observations/actions and checked images go to OpenAI (paid API usage). Clues are limited to 500 characters or 15 seconds. Snapshot search stays stopped during API calls. Live search sends recent frames to a persistent Realtime session, including during movement, at up to your selected image rate. This may increase API usage. Actual uploads and completed assessments are shown separately. Old images are removed from the session; no video is saved locally. GPT proposes a sequence of up to 3 short movements, then looks again; local code checks the whole sequence and every movement. Snapshot search assesses images between sequences. Live assessments can interrupt a sequence for a possible target, uncertainty or a stop request; only fresh stationary assessments authorize new movements or confirm a find. Choose 0–24 search turns and 0–12 Explore steps (defaults 8 and 4). These are maximum budgets, not guaranteed movement counts. The fixed limits remain 6 centring turns, 20 stationary image checks (plus at most 20 background checks in Live mode) and 90 seconds, including clue interpretation. Limits apply to the next search and cannot change while it is running. Search turns last 0.3 seconds, forward steps 0.40 seconds and backward steps 0.30 seconds at your selected speed. Full speed bypasses wheel trim and can travel farther in each burst; test wheel directions with wheels raised first. Centring always uses calibrated speed with 0.12-second turns. Speed resets after each search. Retreat is permitted only within 10 seconds of a completed forward step, with no intervening turn or retreat. Distance is not measured and the rear is unseen. Explore requires a clear floor area checked by you. Two stationary images confirm a find; then it centres and speaks one sentence in an AI-generated voice. No automatic approach or obstacle avoidance. Audio and photos stay in memory on the Pi. STOP, leaving the page, stale decisions, lost video or disconnection stops the search.
