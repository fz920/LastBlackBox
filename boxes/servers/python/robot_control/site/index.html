Connecting to conversation…

Enter to send · Shift+Enter for a new line

Sends your question + a camera image to OpenAI. Replies play through the robot’s mouth.

How conversation works

Speak near the robot while holding the button, then release to send. Up to 15 seconds per recording or 500 characters per typed question. Keyboard: focus Hold to talk and hold Enter.

Typed questions do not record the microphone. End conversation stops the reply and clears its context. Leaving the page cancels the turn. Remembers up to six questions; clears after two idle minutes.

Requires internet and paid API usage. Audio and images stay in memory on the Pi, not in recording files. This mode does not control movement. Preview and offline descriptions stay local.

Connecting to search…

Type a clue or hold the button and speak near the robot, then release to search. It turns to find and centre a match, then speaks. Keep the area clear; no obstacle sensing.

Search limits and privacy

Your typed clue or released recording, search criteria and checked images go to OpenAI (paid API usage). Clues are limited to 500 characters or 15 seconds of recording. The robot stays stopped while interpreting; an unclear clue prompts a question. Finds and clarification questions use an AI-generated voice. Audio and photos stay in memory on the Pi. Search allows at most 8 short search turns, 6 centring turns and 20 image checks within 90 seconds, including recording and interpretation. Search turns last 0.3 seconds; centring turns last 0.12 seconds, at slow speed. A match and a centred view each need two stationary image checks. Uncertain or lost targets stop centring. It does not approach the object; wheel angles and a full-circle sweep are not measured. STOP, leaving the page, lost video or disconnection cancels the search.
